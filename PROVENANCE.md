# Provenance and third-party assets

This is a personal appearance export from a Cursor-hosted Ubuntu 24.04.4 XFCE desktop, captured September 26, 2026 by inspecting the machine through its terminal and remote desktop.

The complete basket export is preserved under `capture/`, including its original compressed archive and all five screenshots. No VM credentials, browser profiles, shell history, or project source directories are part of this appearance export. The Workshop screenshots show a separate trial performed during the same session; Workshop itself is not included.

The capture contains third-party WhiteSur theme/icon/cursor assets, Plank themes, Inter, JetBrains Mono, Public Sans, Source Sans 3, and DejaVu fonts, XFCE assets, application launcher files, Cursor logos, and the original wallpaper. Their respective authors retain their rights. The capture did not include a complete set of upstream license notices, and the wallpaper's original author/license is not established here. No blanket open-source license is granted over the bundled assets; review and obtain applicable notices/permissions before redistributing them publicly.
